## YouTube

Playback Controls

- Spacebar: Play/Pause the video
- K: Play/Pause the video
- J: Rewind 10 seconds
- L: Fast forward 10 seconds
- Arrow Left: Rewind 5 seconds
- Arrow Right: Fast forward 5 seconds
- M: Mute/Unmute the video

Navigation

- F: Enter/Exit Full Screen
- T: Toggle Theater Mode
- C: Toggle Captions/Subtitles
- Numbers (1-9): Jump to a specific percentage of the video (1 = 10%, 2 = 20%, etc.)

Player Controls

- Home: Go to the beginning of the video
- End: Go to the end of the video
- Up Arrow: Increase volume
- Down Arrow: Decrease volume

Miscellaneous

- Focus on the search bar
  - / (Slash)
- Shift + N: Play the next video in the playlist
- Shift + P: Play the previous video in the playlist
